02 / About

Engineering is
the iteration.

I work at the intersection of mechanical design, electronics and control. My projects tend to start as a mechanism, a sketch or a question — then move through CAD, fabrication, wiring, firmware, testing and a few failures.

The goal is not just to make a prototype. It is to understand why it works, where it breaks, and how to make the next version better.

03 / Approach

Built in the
real world.

01

Mechanism first

Start with geometry, loads, motion and constraints before hiding problems behind software.

02

Prototype early

Use fabrication and quick experiments to expose bad assumptions while they are still cheap to fix.

03

Integrate everything

A good robot is a system: mechanical, electrical and software decisions have to work together.
